### Overview
**Danball Senki**, also known as **LBX: Little Battlers eXperience**, is an anime and game series by *Level-5*.
The anime first aired in **2011**. It's set in a future where people battle with **LBX**, small customizable robots that fight inside cardboard arenas called **Diorama Cubes**.

### Main Characters
1. **Ban Yamano** – the main character, who uses the LBX **Achilles**
2. **Amy Kawamura** – Ban's friend, who uses **Kunoichi**
3. **Kaz Aoshima** – Ban's best friend, who uses **Hunter**
4. **Jin Kaidou** – a skilled rival LBX player

### Why I Like It
- Cool robot designs and battles
- You can customize your own LBX
- Strong theme of friendship and teamwork
